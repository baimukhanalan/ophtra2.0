# OPHTRA 2.0 — UI/UX & visual QA audit

Audit date: 2026-09-25 · Build: `vite preview` on http://localhost:4173 · Contract: `DESIGN.md`
Auditor scope: every static route (41 + 404), 15 dynamic samples (3 × doctors / services / knowledge-base / authors / news), `/articles/:slug` redirect, `/media-center?tab=video`; 1440×900, 1024×768, 390×844 in RU; KK + EN spot-check on 5 pages (desktop + mobile); reduced-motion pass (6 pages); interactive states (mega-menus, drawer, language menu, cookie banner, assistant, accessibility panel, booking wizard, form errors/success, account, all 14 admin sections).

## How it was captured

* Scripts: `e2e/visual/capture.mjs` (scrolls in 400 px steps with 420 ms settle, viewport JPEG every ~0.85 vh, DOM checks + axe colour-contrast after full scroll), `e2e/visual/states.mjs` (interactive states), `e2e/visual/sheets.py` (contact sheets), `e2e/visual/summarize.py` (automated findings).
* Raw screenshots: `e2e/results/visual/{ru,kk,en,ru-reduced,ru-auth}/{desktop,tablet,mobile}/<route>__NN.jpg` (≈1 870 RU frames); contact sheets in `…/_sheets/`; interactive states in `e2e/results/visual/states/`; machine summary in `e2e/results/visual/summary-ru.txt` and per-viewport `_report-*.json`.
* All screenshot paths below are relative to the project root.

## Executive summary

The build is close to the Figma's editorial language: forest heroes, cream paper, Source Serif headings over Manrope, gold indices, hairline bento grids and a rich motion system are all present and consistent in header/footer. Page-level horizontal overflow is **zero** on every route and viewport, no broken or stretched images were detected, keyboard focus rings are visible, and every section carries at least one scroll effect.

However, **several Figma errors that DESIGN.md explicitly forbids have come back**, and there are two conversion-breaking defects:

1. **Mobile booking wizard is unusable** — the wizard grid expands to 846 px inside a 352 px card. Option cards and the «Далее» button sit off-screen (x = 777 px), so a phone user cannot get past step 1 (blocker, `/appointment`, all languages).
2. **Every `.oph-select` renders a tiled chevron pattern** over its text ("⌄⌄⌄ Все темы ⌄⌄⌄") — on the International-patients, Science/partnership, Knowledge-base and all admin forms (blocker for the lead forms).
3. **Figma error 4 is back:** every doctor card and doctor-profile hero shows two-letter initials pasted over a green-tinted clinic facade photo (blocker per contract).
4. **Figma error 5 is back:** on first visit the cookie card covers the primary hero CTA «Записаться на консультацию» (desktop) and «Международные пациенты / Второе мнение» (mobile). The two floating buttons (accessibility bottom-left, «Помощник» bottom-right) overlap body copy, cards, CTAs, footer links, the mobile drawer and even the assistant's own «Связаться с оператором» button. Automated checks logged overlaps on 146 of 162 route×viewport captures.
5. **Descender clipping:** the SplitText word mask (`.oph-split-word { overflow:hidden }`) cuts off «у», «р», «д», «щ» descenders in the CTA-band title, the footer mission title and many section headings. Figma error 1 ("clipped at the baseline") is back.
6. **The header account icon is squashed to 6 px wide** on every desktop page (padding bug), so the account entry looks like a blank button.
7. **WCAG AA contrast fails across the site (2 044 axe nodes):** gold `#a88b5e` on cream (2.6–3.0:1) for all section indices and eyebrows, and muted `#6e7671` on cream/paper (3.9–4.4:1) for 11 px labels, leads and stat captions.
8. **About repeats the home page** (Figma error 7): the same care-map bento, science block and mission. The mission statement then appears a third time in the global footer, on every page.

On top of these, the site feels repetitive at scale. The same ring motif appears in 9 heroes, three clinic-facade photos are reused in unrelated contexts, the same StickyStory template is used on six pages, and every section shows both an index label and an identical eyebrow. Mobile pages are very long: International is 26 300 px (33 screens) and the footer alone is five screens. The founder page has no portrait of the founder.

**Counts.** 5 blockers, 26 major, 39 minor, 13 polish (83 numbered findings).

## Severity legend
**blocker** — breaks a key task or violates a "must not reappear" contract item · **major** — clearly visible defect / AA failure / hurts conversion · **minor** — noticeable inconsistency · **polish** — refinement toward the premium Figma feel.
